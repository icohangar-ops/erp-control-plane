# Curation notes

This tree is curated by hand from `genbi/draft/` (generated by `python -m genbi.mdl_gen generate`). Curation decisions for this scope:

- Descriptions: every column carries business language; KPI formulas were transcribed from the dbt SQL headers and verified against the mart SQL.
- Grain: `primary_key` matches the dbt `unique` tests exactly.
- Relationships: inferred from dbt relationship tests (surrogate-key joins only; crosswalk/ref plumbing is outside the modeled set).
- Knowledge: business terms and sample questions in `knowledge/` give the NL layer its vocabulary; terms were verified against KPI formulas.
- Views: read-only pre-joined projections in `views/` (region revenue, latest inventory position).

Serving: the MDL is validated in CI (`python -m genbi.mdl_gen validate`) and consumed by the wren bootstrap service (`docker/compose/genbi/bootstrap/`), which registers the project with WrenAI against the READ_ONLY DuckDB URI from `genbi.connection.duckdb_uri` — see `genbi/mdl/README.md`.
